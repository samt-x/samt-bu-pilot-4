The pilot had four work packages, with one deliverable each. The table shows what was planned and what was actually delivered.

| Work package | Planned | Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Information package | Information package for everyone involved and for system vendors: what the digitalisation of diplomas entails | A presentation for affected actors (Sikt, August 2026). The content has been included in [About pilot 4](../om-pilot-4/), [Results and the way forward](../resultater-og-veien-videre/) and [System vendors](systemleverandorer/) |
| 2. Architecture and information model | Architecture and information model as the basis for the data flow of diplomas and results | [Architecture and information model](arkitektur-og-informasjonsmodell/), report of 30 August 2026, with minutes of the working meetings |
| 3. Legal needs | Overview of legal needs for digitalising and sharing diplomas and results | [Legal needs](juridiske-behov/), legal assessment from Sikt |
| 4. System vendors | Overview of system vendors with contact points | [System vendors](systemleverandorer/), overview of school administration systems as of September 2026 |
