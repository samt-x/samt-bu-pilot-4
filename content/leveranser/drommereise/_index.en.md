The dream journey describes how things should work for the citizen when everything is in place. It guides the work in the pilot.

- All diplomas and individual results from education are gathered in the extended NVB (KREG) and available throughout life.
- Data from the systems of municipalities and county authorities is delivered to KREG, so that all results can be shared and kept securely.
- Digital results are legally equivalent to paper documentation.
- Public agencies and private businesses can retrieve education results easily – with a legal basis or the citizen's consent.
- Citizens have all their results and diplomas in one place and can share them from Vitnemålsportalen (the Norwegian Diploma Registry).

The transition from paper documentation to digital evidence shall give citizens full control of their own information, make it possible for employers, the public sector and others to digitalise and automate processes, and counter fraud.

The name of the Competence Register has not been decided. Early documents in the pilot also use «extended NVB».
