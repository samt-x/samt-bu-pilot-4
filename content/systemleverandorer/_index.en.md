*This page reproduces the pilot's «Oversikt over skoleadministrative systemer» (overview of school administration systems) as of September 2026, the deliverable from work package 4. The overview is to provide contact points for all parties that will deliver data to the Competence Register.*

School administration systems are the primary source of results and diplomas from primary, lower secondary and upper secondary education. The requirements and expectations for the systems are set out in the [Information package](../informasjonspakke/).

## Overview of school administration systems

| System | Vendor | Website | School level | Who uses the system? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (Visma) InSchool | Visma | InSchool | Upper secondary | Public upper secondary schools, some private upper secondary schools, county tertiary vocational colleges |
| iSkole | Barman Hanssen AS | Barman Hanssen AS – iSkole | Primary and lower secondary, upper secondary | Private primary, lower secondary and upper secondary schools |
| IST Everyday | IST | [Primary and lower secondary](https://www.ist.com/no/grunnskole), [upper secondary](https://www.ist.com/no/videreg%C3%A5ende-skole) | Primary and lower secondary, upper secondary | Many municipalities and some private schools |
| Vigo | IST on behalf of Novari IKS | [ist.com/no/vigo](https://www.ist.com/no/vigo) | Upper secondary | Vocational training (apprenticeships), and admission to upper secondary school |
| Vigilo | EG Vigilo | [vigilo.no](https://vigilo.no/) | Primary and lower secondary | 895 schools, the largest municipalities in Norway |
| Flyt skole | Visma Flyt | [minflyt.no](https://minflyt.no/administrative-system/skoleadministrasjon/grunnskole) | Primary and lower secondary | Small and medium-sized municipalities, including Bærum and Kristiansand |
